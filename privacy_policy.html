Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Video Downloader ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

To provide video downloading and status saving services, the app may access the following on your device:

  • Storage & Media Permissions: We require access to your device’s storage to save downloaded videos, audio, and images. On Android 13 and above, we specifically request access to Media Video, Media Audio, and Media Images.
  • Internet Access: To connect to video platforms and download content.
  • Cookies & Session Data: When you log into Instagram or Facebook via the app’s built-in browser, session cookies are stored locally on your device in a private database. This data is used only to authenticate your requests to those platforms and is never sent to our servers.
  • Advertising ID: We may use your device's Advertising ID to serve relevant advertisements.

We do not collect personal information, passwords, or payment information.

How Your Data Is Used

  • Local Processing: All video extraction and merging (via FFmpeg) happen on your device. We do not use external servers to process your downloads.
  • No File Uploads: We do not upload, collect, or store your downloaded files, WhatsApp statuses, or personal media on any external servers. Your files remain 100% under your control on your device.
  • Storage Folders: Downloaded content is saved in professional subfolders titled "Video Downloader" within your device’s Movies, Music, and Pictures directories.

Advertising

This app displays ads served by Google Ad Manager. Google may use your device's Advertising ID and other non-personal data to serve relevant advertisements.

The types of ads shown include:

  • Banner, Interstitial, and Native ads.
  • Rewarded and App Open ads.

You can opt out of personalized ads by visiting your device's Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more information on how Google uses data, visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics & Attribution

We use Apptrove, a mobile measurement platform, to track app installs and in-app events such as document scans, PDF saves, and shares. This helps us understand how users interact with the app and improve our services.

Apptrove may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID / GAID)
  • App usage events (anonymous)
  • Install source information

For more information, visit: https://apptrove.com/privacy-policy

Third-Party Services

The app utilizes several third-party libraries and services to function:

  • Google Play Services: For core app functionality and security.
  • YoutubeDL-Android & FFmpeg: For native video extraction and merging.
  • Glide: For fast loading of media thumbnails.
  • Apptrove (analytics & attribution)
  • Google Ad Manager: For monetization.

User Responsibilities & Copyright

This app is intended for personal use only. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the legal right to download content from third-party platforms. We do not encourage or condone the unauthorized downloading of copyrighted material.

Security

We prioritize the security of your data. By keeping all video processing local to your device and using standard Android security protocols for cookie storage, we minimize the risk of data exposure.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of the app after any changes signifies your acceptance of the new terms.
