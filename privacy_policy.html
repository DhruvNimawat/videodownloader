03 Information We Access & Collect

To deliver core functionality, the App accesses the following on your device:

  • Storage & Media Permissions (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Broad media access is required as a core function of this App — to save user-initiated downloads (videos, audio, images) directly to your device. This permission is used solely for saving content the user explicitly chooses to download. On Android 12 and below, general storage permission (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) is used for the same purpose.
  • Internet Access: Required to connect to video platforms and retrieve content URLs for download.
  • Cookies & Session Data: When you use the App's built-in browser to log into Instagram or Facebook, session cookies are stored locally in a private, encrypted database on your device. This data is never transmitted to BE Global's servers. It is used solely to authenticate your session with those platforms.
  • Advertising ID (GAID): Your device's Advertising ID may be accessed by Google Ad Manager and Apptrove for serving relevant ads and tracking app install attribution.

We do not collect: your name, email address, passwords, payment information, contacts, location data, or any personally identifiable information directly.

04 How Your Data Is Used

  • Local Processing Only: All video extraction, format conversion, and merging operations (performed via YoutubeDL-Android and FFmpeg) occur entirely on your device. No video data is sent to BE Global servers.
  • No File Uploads: We do not upload, collect, store, or transmit your downloaded files, WhatsApp statuses, or personal media to any external server. Your downloaded content remains 100% on your device.
  • Advertising: Advertising ID and device signals are used by Google Ad Manager to display banner, interstitial, native, rewarded, and app open advertisements within the App.
  • Analytics & Attribution: Apptrove receives anonymous usage events (such as app install, download initiated, download completed) and the Advertising ID to measure campaign attribution and understand user interaction with the App.
  • Storage Folders: Downloaded content is saved into clearly labelled sub-folders titled "Video Downloader" within your device's Movies, Music, and Pictures directories.

06 Advertising

This App displays advertisements served by Google Ad Manager. Google may use your device's Advertising ID and other non-personal signals to serve relevant advertisements. Ad types shown include Banner, Interstitial, Native, Rewarded, and App Open ads.

Upon first launch, you will be asked for your consent before personalized advertising is enabled, in accordance with applicable privacy regulations including GDPR and India's DPDP Act.

To opt out of personalized ads at any time, go to your device's Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (or on Android 12+, Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID).

07 User Consent

Before any advertising ID or analytics tracking is activated, the App will display a consent prompt on first launch. You may choose to:

  • Accept personalized ads and analytics: Advertising ID and usage events are shared with Google Ad Manager and Apptrove for personalized ads and attribution tracking.
  • Decline personalized ads: You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. Analytics events will remain anonymous. Your Advertising ID will not be used for personalization.

You can change your consent preferences at any time in the App's Settings screen or via your device's system settings.

08 Data Retention

  • Session Cookies (Instagram/Facebook): Stored locally on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Never sent to BE Global.
  • Downloaded Media Files: Stored on your device indefinitely until you manually delete them.
  • Advertising ID & Analytics Data: Retained by Google Ad Manager and Apptrove per their own retention policies (typically 13–26 months). BE Global does not retain this data independently.

09 Your Right to Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of any data associated with your use of the App. To exercise this right:

  • Session/Cookie Data: Go to your device Settings → Apps → Video Downloader → Clear Data. This immediately deletes all locally stored session cookies.
  • Advertising ID Data: On Android 12+, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID. This resets your Advertising ID and disassociates all prior data.
  • Request via Email: Send a deletion request to kunal@thebeglobal.com. We will process your request and contact relevant third-party partners (Apptrove, Google) within 30 days.
